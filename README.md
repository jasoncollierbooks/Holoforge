# HOLO//FORGE — text to design, in your browser
Type what you want; get a 3D part, an engineer's inspection with weak-point pins, and Lighter / Stronger / Cheaper generations on a branching timeline. Lab Assistant answers design questions (99 built-in entries), converts units, runs calculators, and takes voice. STL export. Assemblies preview (hinge, gear pair, slider).

Live: https://jasoncollierbooks.github.io/holoforge/

Everything runs client-side (three.js). No account, no server. AI is optional: bring your own key, stored only in your browser.
Checks are quick hand-calculation estimates for early design — not a substitute for a licensed engineer or local codes.
