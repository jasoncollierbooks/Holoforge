How to use

  1. Type a part (or tap an example chip) and press FORGE.
  2. Red/amber pins on the model are weak points — read them in Engineer inspection.
  3. Tap Lighter / Stronger / Cheaper to make a new generation. Every version is kept in Generations; click one to go back and branch.
  4. Drag sliders in Dimensions to tweak.
  5. ⤓ STL downloads a file for 3D printing.
  6. Ask the Lab Assistant anything, by typing or 🎙. Or just describe what you need: “a shelf bracket that holds 50 lb, 8 inches deep, aluminum”, then “make it stronger”, “now in steel”, “twice as long”.
  7. Wrong or missing answer? Press ✎ Teach it. 🧠 Meaning loads a small free model into your browser so it understands rephrased questions.

Results are quick hand-calc estimates, not a licensed engineer's sign-off.
